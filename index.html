Garden

The Story

The GARDEN collection is designed for carefree summer days, so that no matter the weather, you can stay vibrant and convey the values of kindness, love, and truth.

As a designer, I didn't want to focus on complex shapes or the conveyance of meaning here; I simply wanted to vividly present the brand's four core words in a summery interpretation.

Brooding Gaze

The Story

This story starts from a lecture about culture at my university. The Necklace of the Dove, or Tawk al-Hamama (The Message of Love and Lovers) is a treatise on love written in 1022 by Ibn Hazm. It was this book that inspired me to create this merch, which tells of two different stages of love: the beginning and the crisis.

The beginning is marked by the gaze that follows his beloved. And I reflected the crisis of words and expressions of love in the form of a binary code that conveys the phrase "I love you" several times and is placed in the form of flowers, which are given to girls as a symbol of love. And so the idea of the collection's design was born.
